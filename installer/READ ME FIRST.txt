CHEST COUNTER - counts the Fortnite chests you open and destroy
===============================================================

HOW TO INSTALL (about 1 minute)

1. Extract this zip somewhere (right-click > Extract All).
2. Double-click "Install Chest Counter.cmd".
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     (This appears because the app is not code-signed by a company. It is safe to skip.)
   - You don't need administrator rights.
3. Chest Counter asks for PERMISSION to read your Fortnite replay folder.
   Click "Allow and start". If you click "Don't allow", it reads nothing and closes.
4. In Fortnite, turn on:  Settings > Game > Replays > Record Replays: ON
   Without replays there is nothing to count.

Prefer not to install? You can also just double-click ChestCounter.exe.

WHAT IT DOES
- Reads only the replay files Fortnite saves on YOUR PC
  (%LOCALAPPDATA%\FortniteGame\Saved\Demos), read-only.
- Shows a small widget (total chests, last match, sessions, games played)
  and a full dashboard in your browser at http://localhost:48231
- Chests are added a few seconds after each match ends.

WHAT IT NEVER DOES
- Never touches Fortnite, its memory or its files, never sends inputs.
  Nothing that can affect your account or anti-cheat.
- Never uses the internet. Your stats stay on your PC
  (%LOCALAPPDATA%\ChestCounter).

TIPS
- Use "Windowed Fullscreen" in Fortnite to see the widget over the game.
- Right-click the widget for Settings, keep-on-top, opacity and more.
- To uninstall: "Uninstall Chest Counter.cmd" (your stats are kept unless you
  choose to delete them).
