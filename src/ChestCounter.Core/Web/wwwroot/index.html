How chests are counted

Chest Counter only reads the replay files Fortnite saves in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FortniteGame\Saved\Demos. It never touches the game, its memory, its files or its network traffic.

  • Opened chests – every chest replicates who searched it (SearchingPawn) when it becomes searched (bAlreadySearched). A chest counts for you when the game names your character.
  • Destroyed chests – when a chest is broken instead of opened (bDestroyed), the game credits whoever broke it, including chests inside a building you smash. Each chest is counted individually, never per building, and a chest that was opened is never counted again as destroyed.
  • Gum Gum Devil Fruit – Fortnite's internal name for it is TackyGoofiness. A destroyed chest credited to you while the fruit was your equipped item is labelled as a Gum Gum destruction. Destructions the game does not credit to anyone are never counted.
  • Eligible matches – only public Battle Royale and Zero Build playlists played since the season start date (the date recorded inside the replay). Everything else is excluded.
  • Pickaxe-only matches – matches where the replay shows you only ever held a pickaxe and got no chests (AFK, spectating, or eliminated before looting) are not counted, so they don't lower your average. This can be turned off in Settings.
  • CPG = total chests ÷ eligible matches analysed. Total chests = opened + destroyed.
  • Treasures – the season's treasure hunt gives you a treasure handler (GA_TreasureHandler_*) every time you receive a treasure, and the replay records the moment. A treasure is yours when the chest opened or broken at that moment is yours. The hunt also shares every treasure a teammate finds with the whole team: those are shown separately and never added to your total. Golden prize chests do not record who triggered them, so they are not counted.
